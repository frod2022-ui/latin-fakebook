3) FLUTE AND PERCUSSION — samples/flute/, samples/perc/
   VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios (https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE)
   License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication) — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Used: flute sustain with vibrato; claves, conga, quinto, tumba, cowbell, güiro, bass drum, snare taps and
   snares-off hits, tambourine shake. Bongo sounds are re-pitched quinto/conga hits; timbales use snares-off snare hits.

The shaker, count-in click and reverb are generated by the app itself.
